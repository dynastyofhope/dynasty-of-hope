    About the competition

    Football for Community Development

    A free-to-enter community tournament harnessing the energy of young people in Goni Gora, encouraging peaceful coexistence and putting local talent in front of scouts and coaches.

    Road to the trophy

    16-Team Knockout Fixtures

    Straight knockout: Round of 16 → Quarter-finals → Semi-finals → Grand Final.

    Competition rules

    • All matches played at Goni Gora Community Field unless announced otherwise.
    • Kick-off times: 10:00am and 2:00pm on match days.
    • 90 minutes (45 × 2). Level at full time → penalties (extra time in the final).
    • Only accredited players on the submitted squad list may feature.
    • Two substitutions maximum per team per match.
    • Walkover awarded if a team fails to appear within 15 minutes of kick-off.

    🎤 Award presentation programme

    Sun 4 Oct 2026 — 2:00pm
    Grand Final kicks off at the community field.
    4:00pm — Final whistle
    Teams assemble at the centre circle; officials submit the match report.
    4:20pm — Medals
    Runners-up receive silver medals and the ₦50,000 cheque.
    4:40pm — Champions
    Trophy lift, gold medals and the ₦100,000 cheque, presented by the Foundation and the community leader.
    5:10pm — Individual awards
    MVP, Highest Goal Scorer, Best Goalkeeper, Best Coach, Fair Play Team and Best Referee.
    5:40pm — Closing remarks
    Foundation address, vote of thanks, group photographs and refreshments.

    🎁 Also on the day

    • Participation certificates for all 16 clubs.
    • Footballs and training bibs donated to community youth groups.
    • Free medical checks and first-aid awareness session.
    • Talent scouting forms for outstanding players.
    • Refreshments for players, officials and spectators.
